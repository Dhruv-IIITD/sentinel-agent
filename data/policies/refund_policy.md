# Refund & Cancellation Policy

Source document for the policy-RAG layer (Phase 2). The agent retrieves the
relevant clause at runtime to decide whether an action needs human approval and
to justify the approval request.

## Refunds

- Refunds of **$50.00 or less** may be issued automatically by the agent.
- Refunds **greater than $50.00** require **manager approval** before issuing.
- Refunds are only permitted **within 30 days** of the delivery date. Requests
  outside this window require manager approval regardless of amount.
- A single order may not be refunded more than once (the original charge is the
  ceiling). Partial refunds are allowed up to the order amount.
- Damaged or defective items are eligible for a full refund within the 30-day
  window, subject to the approval thresholds above.

## Cancellations

- Orders that have **not yet shipped** may be cancelled automatically.
- Orders **in transit** require manager approval to cancel.
- Delivered orders cannot be cancelled — handle as a return/refund instead.

## Store credit

- Store credit up to **$100.00** may be applied automatically as a goodwill
  gesture. Amounts above $100.00 require manager approval.

## Hard limits (never auto-approved, regardless of the above)

- No single refund or credit above **$1,000.00** may be issued without
  **director** approval.
